## Quality Checks

- There is one primary player, not several incompatible audiences merged together.
- The promise describes why the player cares; the core loop describes what the player does.
- Hypotheses can be disproved by observable evidence.
- The first playable does not silently include every planned system.
- Exclusions remove ambiguity and expensive premature work.
- Success does not depend only on a score or the designer's preference.
- Human confirmation is explicit and scoped.
